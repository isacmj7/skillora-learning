### M1 Role targeting

**Q-C4-M1-01 "Why Senior Python Backend or AI Engineer, and which do you want more?"** Medium. `career.role-targeting`. Indian-product, AI-startup, global-product. Goal `B:S A:S H:S`.
- Outline: clear primary and secondary; 2-3 evidence items per target; what each is judged on (reliability and ownership vs LLM quality, evals, cost); a real gap and a plan; link to the employer's product; shows the two are coherent (backend depth makes LLM features production-ready); asks what success looks like at six months.
- Weak: "Both, I'm flexible"; AI hype; calling backend boring; no gap.
- Probes: What makes you choose backend if offered both? What have you shipped where LLM quality, not just API calls, mattered? How do you evaluate an LLM feature?
- Senior-bar: mid lists skills; senior ties choice to evidence and opportunity cost; staff states what scope they would own in 12 months and how they would measure it.

**Q-C4-M1-02 "How do you tell whether an 'AI Engineer' job is real engineering or API integration?"** Medium-hard. `career.role-targeting`. global-IT-consulting, AI-startup. Goal `B:N A:M H:M`.
- Outline: reads JD for ownership signals (evals, retrieval, latency, cost, monitoring); training vs API use; questions for the hiring manager on data, evals, on-call; integration work can still be valuable; links to own growth goals; defines what they would accept.
- Weak: all AI roles are hype; or all are deep ML.
- Probes: Which questions would you ask? What is a red flag? Would you take an integration-heavy role, and under what conditions?
- Senior-bar: staff adds how to probe the team's eval and incident practice before accepting.

**Q-C4-M1-03 "What do you bring from 11 years that a four-year Python specialist doesn't?"** Medium. `career.role-targeting`. IT-services, Big4, global-product. Goal `B:S A:S H:S`. (Delivery drilled in C1.M9; here the evidence content is scored.)
- Outline: concrete cross-stack lessons (incidents, data modelling, release practice); judgement over syntax; mentoring or cross-team example; Python depth being actively built, with specifics; connects to the role; no age or tenure comparisons; under two minutes.
- Weak: "Experience matters more"; vague adjectives; slights peers.
- Probes: Give one decision that changed because of past experience. Where are you still learning in Python?
- Senior-bar: staff shows decisions that scaled beyond own code (standards, hiring, incident reviews).

### M2 Positioning thesis and domain-switch rationale

**Q-C4-M2-01 "Give me your positioning thesis in one paragraph."** Medium. `career.positioning-thesis`. all. Goal `B:S A:S H:S`.
- Outline: who you are now (role and scope); two or three proof points you can defend with your personal part; why this switch and why now; the gap named and being closed; what you want next; under 120 words; consistent with the Profile Facts Ledger if used.
- Weak: adjective list; chronology; unverifiable metrics.
- Probes: Which proof point would you drop if challenged? What is the personal contribution within the team result?
- Senior-bar: staff thesis is falsifiable and names a measurable first-year outcome.
- ref: spoken 3-minute walk-through -> C1.M9; "Why are you leaving" -> C1.M7/M9.

**Q-C4-M2-02 (ref: C1.M9-Q1) "Why have you only recently moved to Python after so many years?"** Medium-hard. `career.domain-switch`. global-product, Indian-product. Goal `B:M A:S H:M`.
- Pointer item: the spoken answer is owned and scored in C1.M9-Q1 (Python-years-versus-seniority). Here the learner only writes the one-sentence switch rationale that goes into the thesis, checked against the evidence list in the Positioning Pack.
- Probes: Which proof point backs the sentence? What would you cut if challenged?
- Senior-bar: staff names the evidence that makes the sentence falsifiable.

**Q-C4-M2-03 "Write the single sentence in your thesis that states the through-line across your domains. How do you test that it is not retrofitted?"** Hard. `career.domain-switch`. Big4, global-IT-consulting. Goal `B:S A:S H:S`. (The spoken drift-versus-thread answer is owned by C1.M9-Q2; this item scores the written artefact.)
- Outline: the truthful constant stated as a testable claim; each move mapped to a scope increase with one dated fact from the Profile Facts Ledger; falsifiability test (name a domain or role where the claim would fail); what to omit from the Positioning Pack; consistency with the profile and portfolio (M3).
- Weak: retrofitted narrative; denies opportunism; no evidence.
- Probes: Which domain contradicts the sentence? What would a recruiter skim see first?
- Senior-bar: staff names how the claim would be tuned per employer cluster without untruth.

**Q-C4-M2-04 "You built a chatbot and a voice agent. How is that different from an AI Engineer's work?"** Hard. `career.domain-switch`. AI-startup, global-product. Goal `B:N A:M H:M`.
- Outline: exactly what was built and personal part; API orchestration vs model work, honestly; production concerns handled (latency, cost, failures, evaluation, safety); what has not been done (training, large-scale eval) without apology; plan to close the gap; observations from real usage; no buzzwords.
- Weak: overclaiming; vague system; no failures discussed.
- Probes: How did you evaluate quality? What broke in production? What would more budget change?
- Senior-bar: staff quantifies trade-offs (latency budget, cost per conversation) without inventing numbers.

**Q-C4-M2-05 "How would you convince us a backend developer can become a strong AI engineer?"** Medium. `career.domain-switch`. Indian-product, AI-startup. Goal `B:N A:M H:M`.
